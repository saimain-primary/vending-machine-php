# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers browsing a vending machine catalog on the `/` page to find and buy products. They want a quick, frictionless purchase — scan what's available, see prices and stock at a glance, tap buy and move on. No extended browsing session; high intent, low patience.

## Product Purpose

A digital vending machine POS system. Customers browse a live product catalog, check stock and pricing, and complete purchases in as few steps as possible. Admins manage inventory separately. Success looks like: customer sees product, customer buys product, transaction completes.

## Brand Personality

Clean, reliable, efficient. No personality theatrics — the interface gets out of the way and lets the transaction happen. Trustworthy like a well-maintained machine.

## Anti-references

- Not a food-delivery app (no hero imagery overload, no promotional banners)
- Not generic SaaS-blue boilerplate
- Not cluttered (no unnecessary chrome, badges, or micro-copy)
- Not playful or loud — this is utility, not entertainment

## Design Principles

1. **Transaction first.** Every element earns its place by moving the user toward a purchase. Decoration is noise.
2. **Stock is status.** Availability is the most important product attribute — make it immediately readable without decoding.
3. **No dead ends.** Out-of-stock products are visible but clearly non-interactive; users never hit a wall.
4. **Density without clutter.** Customers should see enough products per screen to browse efficiently without feeling overwhelmed.
5. **Instant trust.** Price, stock, and the buy action must be unambiguous. Ambiguity kills conversions at a vending machine.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text for outdoor/bright-light environments (vending machines are often in well-lit spaces). Touch-friendly tap targets for kiosk-style or mobile use.
